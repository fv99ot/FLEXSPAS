<product_requirements>
The objective is to establish a comprehensive check-in and management system for Flex Spa Los Angeles. Key functionalities include:
1.  **User Management**: Role-based login (Managers, Employees), customer search, addition, notes, banning, and detailed profiles with visit history and membership status.
2.  **Membership**: QR code-based registration for pending customer approvals; membership validation during check-in.
3.  **Check-in/Checkout**: Room/locker assignment, 8-hour session limit, 0/hour (rounded up) overtime payment system, and an option to pay overtime now or as IOU.
4.  **Payments**: Finalization with dynamic additional items and whole-amount discounts; integrated receipt printing.
5.  **Reporting**: End-of-shift sales with payment method breakdown.
6.  **UI/UX**: Flex Spas branding (logo image replacing text), two-line tab layout, room map displaying remaining time, click-to-manage room/locker (renew/upgrade/checkout prompts, with base rate for renewal and difference for upgrade), consistent dark theme (bluish-black background, black header, blood-red active tabs/content areas), visible input text, and consistent button styling.
7.  **Advanced Features**: 3-column, room-type-specific waitlist allowing multiple entries, password management, employee management with assign locker option, and static QR code generation.
</product_requirements>
